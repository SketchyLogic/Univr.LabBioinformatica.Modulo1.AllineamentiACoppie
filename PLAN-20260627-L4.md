## 1. One-paragraph summary

L4 is the direct sequel to L3. Where L3 built global alignment (Needleman–Wunsch), L4 
- **fixes NW's blind spot** — it ignored how indels should be penalized — with the **Waterman–Smith affine gap penalty** `w(k)=g+e(k−1)`;
- introduces **local alignment** (**Smith–Waterman**, 1981) and the global-vs-local distinction, the workhorse of database search (BLAST);
- asks *is a score meaningful?* — **statistical significance** via the **Z-score** (global, by shuffling) and the **Karlin–Altschul E-value** with its **Gumbel/extreme-value** distribution (local);
- spends its second half on **substitution/scoring matrices** — how amino-acid similarity is quantified, the **PAM** family (Dayhoff's accepted point mutations, matrix multiplication `(PAM1)ⁿ`, log-odds scores `s=10·log(q/p)`), the **BLOSUM** family (BLOCKS, BLOSUM62), **PAM vs BLOSUM**, and the **twilight zone** (~20–25 % identity, where homology becomes undetectable). It cleanly fills the three topics L3's source map explicitly **deferred**: Smith–Waterman/local, BLAST context, and gap-open/extend detail.
